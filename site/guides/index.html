Guides in preparation

These are the guides currently being written. Each one answers a single question: what should you actually buy, and what should you ignore?

How our guides work

Every guide follows the same structure:

  • The question. What you are actually trying to decide.
  • The specs that matter. The numbers that change the experience, explained without jargon.
  • The specs that do not. What the marketing sheet emphasises and why it rarely matters.
  • Price bands. What you get at each tier, and where the value drops off.
  • What we would buy. A clear recommendation, with the trade-off stated.

Guides are based on published specifications and aggregated reviews from other outlets. We do not claim to have physically tested products unless stated explicitly.
