How we reach a verdict

Every review follows the same structure, so you can compare across articles:

  • What it is. The product, its price bracket and who it is aimed at.
  • Specs that matter. The numbers that actually change the experience, not the marketing sheet.
  • How it compares. Direct comparison against the two or three obvious alternatives at the same price.
  • Who should buy it. And, just as important, who should not.
