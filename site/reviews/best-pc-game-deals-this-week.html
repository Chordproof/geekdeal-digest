How we verify prices

Every deal on this page comes from a live store feed. We then check the sale price against the store's own list price — not a third-party estimate, not a historical average. The discount percentage is calculated from those two numbers.

Each card carries a verification timestamp. Deals older than 24 hours get an amber marker. Deals older than 48 hours get a red marker, which means the price may have changed and you should confirm before buying.

We do not publish a deal we have not verified. If a price looks too good to be true, it usually is — and we would rather miss a deal than send you to a dead link.
