Report an outdated deal

Prices change fast, and sometimes our pipeline catches a price that has already moved. Every deal card has a "Report outdated" button — use it and we will re-check the listing on the next run.

If the button is not working for you, email us with the deal title and the store, and we will look at it manually.

Corrections and editorial

If we got a specification wrong, recommended something that does not deserve it, or missed a better alternative, we want to know. Send the article URL and what should change.
