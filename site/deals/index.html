How this list is built

Our pipeline runs every 6 hours and applies four gates before a deal reaches this page:

  • Real discount. The offer price must be below the list price reported by the same store. No baseline, no listing.
  • Reasonable price history. A deal that is only "cheap" because the list price was inflated last week is discarded.
  • Correct market. US and Canada only. A price that only exists in another region is not a deal for our readers.
  • Working link. If the store link does not resolve, the deal is dropped instead of published broken.

We do not claim to open the retailer's page or check stock. What we do claim is that the price and the timestamp are real.
